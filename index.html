How it works

From signal to story.

Why this exists

I built Briefroom because I sat in the same editorial meeting you're sitting in right now.

I ran editorial and commercial operations for an international publisher for ten years. We had hundreds of signals arriving daily. We had good editors and we still missed stories. Not because the team wasn't capable, but because the volume was simply not processable by humans. That gap is where Briefroom was born.

I watched editors spend their best hours filtering rather than writing. I watched stories get missed because nobody had time to check what competitors had covered. I watched briefs get built from scratch, every single time, when most of the work was the same work we'd done the day before.

Briefroom is the system I wished I'd had. Built from the inside out.
